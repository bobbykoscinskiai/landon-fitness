About

Built Different, Driven by Purpose

Landon Fankhauser is an 18-year-old fitness creator, faith-driven motivator, and Exercise Science student at Grand Valley State University. A former varsity baseball player at Buchanan High School and crowned Mr. Buchanan 2025, Landon brings that same competitive discipline to the gym and to everyone he coaches.

His focus is simple: help real people build lean muscle, dial in their nutrition, and become the fittest version of themselves — no gimmicks, just consistency and the right mindset.

Faith is the foundation. Landon is an active Christian who shares scripture and biblical encouragement across his platforms, because he believes the mental and spiritual side is just as important as the physical.

The Foundation

Three Pillars That Define the Grind

GYM

Discipline Over Motivation

The iron never lies. Every rep, every set, every early morning session builds more than muscle — it builds character. Landon's fitness journey is about showing up when it's hard and pushing through when it hurts.

GOD

Faith as Foundation

Before the weights, before the content, there's faith. Landon's relationship with God is the bedrock of everything he does. It's the quiet strength behind the loud ambition, the peace that fuels the grind.

MINDSET

Think It, Become It

Your mind is the most powerful muscle. Landon believes that the right mindset can transform your body, your relationships, and your entire life. Don't over complicate it — just try, stay consistent, and keep going.
